Probability that the player scores a rushing or receiving touchdown, assuming he plays — the same basis on which these markets settle, so a quarterback counts for his rushing touchdowns (each team's expected starter is listed; for him Opp/gm is carries). Driven by projected volume and the market's implied team total, and for quarterbacks also goal-line carries and how often they have scored; otherwise the red zone and goal-line columns are context, not model inputs, because adding them was tested and gained nothing.
