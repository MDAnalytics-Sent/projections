Anytime Touchdown — 321 players
Probability that the player scores a rushing or receiving touchdown, assuming he plays — the same basis on which these markets settle, so a quarterback counts for his rushing touchdowns (each team's expected starter is listed; for him Opp/gm is carries). Driven by projected volume and the market's implied team total, and for quarterbacks also goal-line carries and how often they have scored; otherwise the red zone and goal-line columns are context, not model inputs, because adding them was tested and gained nothing. Gm is games played this season. Probabilities above about 45% have run a few points high in backtesting. Players ruled out or doubtful, on injured reserve, waived or left off the game-day active list are dropped as soon as that becomes public, which lifts the share of listed players who actually take a snap from 83% to 97%. Q marks a questionable player and Q·DNP one who also missed practice all week — those have played only about half the time.
Receiving Yards — 276 players
Projected yards assuming the player plays, with the historically observed rate of clearing each line for a projection of this size. Over probabilities are fit on prior seasons only and run about 1-2 points high.
No leakage. Each prediction uses only games that had finished before that game's kickoff, plus completed prior seasons. Until kickoff it updates automatically as new results arrive; at kickoff it is locked permanently and never recomputed. Both model regimes and the confidence curve are fit only on 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025.
Model. This week uses EPA plus this season's point differential.
Confidence is the historically observed hit rate for predictions of this size, fit as a smooth curve. Games near the bottom really are coin tosses.
Caveat. This model does not beat the betting market: walk-forward over 2017-2025 it called 64.6% of games straight up (Brier 0.221) against 67.1% (Brier 0.210) for the closing moneyline favourite, and it has no edge against the spread.
