Anytime Touchdown — top 100 of 1254 players
Chance the player scores a rushing or receiving touchdown, assuming he plays. Built from his carries, catches, yards and touchdowns this season (last season counts as two games early on), his share of his team's touches and the scoring matchup, all as of kickoff; fit on prior seasons only. Out of sample on 2025 it was well calibrated: players given 26% scored 27% of the time; chances above 60% ran a few points high. Listed players touched the ball in their team's previous game. Fair odds = 1 ÷ chance.
No leakage. Every rating uses only games finished before that game's kickoff, plus completed prior seasons. The rating-to-points mapping and the confidence curve are fit on 2016-2025 alone (7,056 games). Until kickoff a prediction updates as results arrive; at kickoff it is locked permanently and never recomputed.
Model. A team's rating blends its games so far this season with a preseason prior. The prior is fit on prior seasons from last year's rating, the four-year recruiting composite, and how much on-field production the roster kept or imported through the transfer portal. Early weeks lean on the prior; by November it is almost all on-field EPA.
Accuracy. Walk-forward over 2018-2025 (5,620 FBS matchups) the model called 71.6% correctly with an average margin error of 13.4 points. Recruiting and transfer terms are worth about a point of accuracy overall and three in the opening weeks. Since v2 the rating-to-points slope is steeper in weeks 1-4, which fixed the early-season underconfidence (it said 68% in weeks 1-2 and won 75%).
Scope. FBS against FBS only. Games against FCS opponents still inform the ratings but are not shown, because a 99% pick tells you nothing.
