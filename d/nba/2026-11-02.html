First FG excludes free throws; first score counts them. 99.9% of first field goals are scored by a starter, and a free throw opens the game 7.1% of the time. Each number is the man's own history shrunk toward his positional rate, times his share of his team's last 20 games — so it is the chance he takes the first basket, not the chance he takes it if he starts. One first basket is scored per game, so where both fives are known the ten are scaled to sum to 100%: built separately they came to 103.8% on average and as much as 128%, which reads as value against a book price that is not there. Once a line-up is posted, about an hour before tip-off, the five are taken as given and nobody else is listed. Rosters come from the team feed, because the season files that record who played are published a season behind. Walk-forward on 1,479 games from 2023 the top pick hit 12.9%, against 10% for picking a starter at random, and a game's stated chances sum to 1.02. A team strength term was tested and earned zero weight.
No leakage. Ratings use only games finished before the slate; the mapping and the confidence curve are fit on earlier seasons alone (12,000 games).
Accuracy. 65.1% of games called correctly, average margin error 10.9 points. Rest is in the model: a team on zero or one day's rest averages 1.95 points worse, against plus 0.43 on two or more, which is a larger swing than the 2.14-point home advantage.
